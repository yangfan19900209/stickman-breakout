```text
Create a production sprite sheet for the existing black-and-white thick-line comic underwater side-scroller shown in the references. This is a NEW SMALL PILOTED SUBMARINE mini-boss, NOT a swimming person and not a giant battleship. Exact layout TWO columns by TWO rows, four complete sprites on actual transparent background, generous transparent gaps and outer margins. Four states row-major: top-left pristine hull, propeller blades one rotation; top-right same pristine hull, propeller blades at a second visibly different rotation, pilot slightly grips wheel tighter; bottom-left same hull with TWO clear cracks and one small dent, pilot still seated, same scale; bottom-right defeated flooded hull with a broken side panel and sagging propeller, pilot still visible unharmed, no flying detached pieces. Damaged states do not grow or shrink and preserve the same hull envelope and emitter locations. No background, water, bubbles, motion trails, text, symbols, labels, grids, beams or explosions. Use the thick contour and quiet white surfaces of the references; simple cohesive comic game art. Each sprite isolated completely for atlas cropping.
```
